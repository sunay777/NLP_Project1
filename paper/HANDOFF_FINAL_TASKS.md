Facts for the checklist (check each against the actual question wording):

- **Claims / limitations:** the limitations are in the Discussion: prompt-real regime only (the
  full-LM runs show the circuit degrading without real prompts), a 35k-parameter model, 16 symbols,
  5-10 seeds, at most 20 generations, and a law that failed its pre-registered extrapolation.
- **Theory:** no theorems. The inheritance law is an empirical fit.
- **Reproducibility / code and data:** public repo `github.com/sunay777/NLP_Project1`.
  `README.txt` has one command per experiment and analysis; `experiments/jobs_v2.py` lists every
  v2 job; seeds are fixed and runs are deterministic on CPU. All data is synthetic, generated by
  `src/data.py`. Pre-registration: `paper/PREDICTION.md` was committed before the confirmatory runs,
  and the commit timestamp shows this.
- **Statistics:** mean +/- sd over seeds (5-10 per cell); 95% bootstrap CIs (1000 draws) for the
  law's parameters; held-out fractions for the law.

  about 17 h of wall time on 9 parallel workers (10-core Mac). Ask Sunay for his share's time.

  task follows Singh et al. (2024); the code is our own and uses PyTorch, NumPy and Matplotlib.
  Broader impact: model collapse and data poisoning (the poison-pulse experiment) matter for models
