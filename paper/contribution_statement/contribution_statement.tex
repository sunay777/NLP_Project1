\textbf{Project:} \emph{Interpretability of Model Collapse} (working title: \emph{Collapse around the circuit: induction heads under recursive training})

\textbf{Research question.} A small transformer learns the induction-head symbol task of
Singh et al.\ (2024) and is then trained recursively on data generated by its previous
generation (Shumailov et al., 2024). Does collapse degrade the induction circuit itself, or
only what the circuit is asked to produce? How does the fraction of real data mixed into each
generation change this, in both the base and the extended version of the task?
