## 1. Introduction (≈ 1 paragraph)
- ICL via induction heads (Olsson 2022; Singh 2024) + model collapse under recursive training (Shumailov 2023/2024).
- Question: **when a model is recursively trained on its own outputs, does collapse happen *in* the ICL circuit, or *around* it?**
- Contributions:
  1. Mechanistic probe (A) tracked every generation, validated causally by ablation.
  2. Real-data dose-response (C) with a real-data-only control that separates recursion from finite-sample effects.
  3. Headline: **dissociation** — the free generative choice degrades in a graded, dose-dependent way while the induction circuit is intact at every real-data fraction.
  4. Methodological: marginal diversity is blind to this collapse; attention-pattern probes give false "circuit collapse" readings because fresh models pick among three distinct algorithms.

## 2. Background (≈ 1 short paragraph)
- Induction circuit = previous-token head (L0) → induction head (L1) via K-composition (Olsson 2022; Singh 2024 Fig. 1).
- Collapse = tails lost, distribution contracts towards modes; statistical-approximation error compounds (Shumailov 2024).
- Circuit identification by attention patterns + ablation (Conmy 2023 as the automated generalisation).

## 3. Method
**Task [V].** Study→query ICL task: K=4 distinct symbols (of 16), per-sequence random labels (of 6); study block = pairs in one order, query block = same symbols in an independent order (variable match offset → only content-based induction solves it). Base predicts each query label; Extended also predicts a free next symbol sq2 (uniform over the K in-context symbols) and its label lq2.
- Why this design (one sentence each; detail in supplement): sparse-signal trap (~0.29), positional shortcut (copy 1.0, zero content matching), direct-match off-by-one head (~0.5); RoPE + tied embeddings + cosine LR decay all needed.

**Model [V].** 2-layer attention-only transformer, d=64, 4 heads, RoPE, tied embeddings, ~35.6k params; AdamW, lr 1e-3, warmup 200, cosine decay.
- **Hyper-parameters [TODO: fill from `results/tuning_*.json`]**: grid lr ∈ {3e-4, 1e-3, 3e-3} × warmup ∈ {100, 200} × d_model ∈ {32, 64}, 2 seeds, selected by validation loss, reported once on a disjoint test split.

**Collapse pipeline [V].** Gen 0 on fresh real data; gen g ≥ 1 trains a *fresh* model on a 20k pool = `real_fraction` real + rest generated by gen g−1 (T=1, every answer slot generated: `--gen_queries all`); 90/10 train/val split of each pool; test = fresh real data. Convergence gate with ≤3 retries (extended seeds can fail to transition at all). 6 generations × fractions {0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1} × 3 seeds × {base, extended}.

**Metrics.** Conditional diversity = mean over contexts of H(p(sq2|context) over the K in-context symbols)/ln K; off-context mass = probability on symbols not in the context; marginal diversity as a foil. Circuit: copy-mass of the best L1 head (canonical + shifted), zero-ablation of all of L1 (variant-agnostic causal dependence), OV label-copy sign per head. Sanity check: extended loss floor ln K/(K+2).

## 5. Discussion (the 20%)
- **Graded, not critical.** No phase transition in real_fraction: both collapse signals are monotone in the real-data share; ≈ 50% real data already matches the all-real control for diversity, while off-context mass keeps improving up to 100%.
- **Recursion vs finite data.** Even all-real data drops conditional diversity 0.994 → ~0.90 once gen ≥ 1 trains on a finite pool; recursion adds a further ~0.08 at rf = 0 on top. This is Shumailov's statistical approximation error (one step) vs its compounding (recursion). Reporting against the rf = 1 control is essential.
- **Dissociation (the answer to the brief's question).** Query labels are fixed by the context, so every generated sequence re-validates the copy algorithm; sq2 is under-determined, so the model's sampling noise feeds back into its own prior. Collapse lives in the under-determined generative choice, not the ICL algorithm. The novel failure mode is *off-context hallucination* (probability on symbols never shown), not loss of the circuit.
- **Why the marginal metric misses it.** In-context symbols are a uniform draw, so per-context sharpening averages out globally; audits of ICL-capable models need conditional diagnostics.
- **Circuit multiplicity (original finding).** Fresh models choose among three algorithms: canonical copy, shifted copy (via the next token, needs a t−2 head), and elimination (attend away from the match and suppress non-matching labels; OV copy −15.8). 10/90 extended and 6/90 base generations used a non-canonical variant; the legacy probe reported false "circuit collapse" in 9 and 6 of them. Attention-pattern probes are algorithm-specific; causal (ablation) measures are needed before claiming a circuit degraded.
- **Limitations:** tiny model/vocab; 6 generations; 3 seeds; T = 1 only; fresh model per generation; zero- (not mean-) ablation; gen-1 init seeds shared across fractions (common random numbers — good for paired comparison, but the seed-2 gen-1 elimination model appears in every fraction); the direction of the sq2 sharpening (what it is keyed on) is not identified — study-slot and query-slot entropies stay ~1.
- Optional add-on: seed a symbol bias at gen 0 (data-poisoning framing) and measure amplification.
